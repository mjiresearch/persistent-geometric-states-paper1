# Stage 9 Candidate A4 — external-constraint audit

Candidate A4 records what independent observations can and cannot currently fix in the A0-A3 advected-state route.

## Physical linear-response parameters after A3

After field-normalization redundancy is removed, the observable weak-field sector is described by:

- a universal stress/curvature scale `Lambda_T^4`;
- an overall dynamical response `A_dyn = beta (a-b)`;
- a lensing response `A_lens = beta b`, or equivalently `r_lens = A_lens/A_dyn`;
- the state-frame/aether kinetic coefficients controlling propagation, stability, and preferred-frame effects.

These are universal parameters. They may not be inferred from Milky Way rotation residuals, Portail17/Hunter24 halo solutions, orbit weights, SPARC, or another galaxy-specific target.

## Independent constraints that do apply

Any Einstein-aether-like realization must respect the observed near-luminal tensor-wave speed. GW170817/GRB170817A constrains the fractional tensor-speed difference to the few-times-10^-15 level. In standard Einstein-aether notation this forces the tensor-speed combination `c13` extremely close to zero.

Preferred-frame PPN parameters are also tightly bounded. Current reviews quote approximately `|alpha_1^PPN| <~ 1e-4` and `|alpha_2^PPN| <~ 1e-7`. Binary-pulsar observations further restrict viable strong-field parameter space.

The observable metric coupling must also satisfy equivalence-principle, lensing/slip, gravitational-wave and cosmological consistency. These constraints can restrict the state-frame kinetic coefficients and the relative conformal/disformal response.

## What those constraints do not yet determine

A2 deliberately deposits persistence through `D_U T`, not through `T` itself. Therefore a static/comoving weak-field system has

    D_U T = 0,

and supplies no direct calibration of the A2 source amplitude. Solar-System and laboratory systems can still constrain preferred-frame behavior and observable metric coupling, but they do not by themselves fix `Lambda_T^4` or `A_dyn` in this derivative-source theory.

Likewise, the GW tensor-speed constraint does not determine the persistent-state normalization.

Consequently, `Lambda_T^4` and `A_dyn` remain non-identifiable until the parent theory supplies an independent dynamical calibration, most naturally through a solved cosmological/background branch or another non-galactic time-dependent system. `r_lens` must be fixed independently from lensing/PPN/cosmological consistency.

## Stage 9 consequence

Passing PPN/GW/equivalence-principle tests is necessary but not sufficient to reopen Milky Way force generation. Candidate A4 blocks the A0-A3 Milky Way acceleration calculation until:

1. external consistency checks are passed;
2. a cosmological/background solution is derived;
3. `Lambda_T^4`, `A_dyn`, and `r_lens` are fixed independently;
4. those values are frozen before halo comparison.

This is an identifiability result, not a failure of the entire persistence framework. It prevents external consistency bounds from being misused as an amplitude calibration.
