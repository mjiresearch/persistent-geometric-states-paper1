# Stage 9 referee-driven theory constraints

This document treats the referee report on *Finite-Memory Gravitational Response from Retarded Green-Function Tails* as a hard constraint set for all subsequent Stage 9 work.

## 1. Conceptual status: effective modification, not exact reformulation

The referee's controlling objection is correct: if the theory is dynamically identical to GR for fixed matter content and boundary data, it cannot produce different galaxy rotation curves, lensing, cosmological growth, or gravitational-wave dispersion.

Stage 9 must therefore use the following interpretation:

> The finite-memory sector is a covariant, strictly retarded **effective macroscopic modification of the curvature-response law inspired by GR tail structure**. It is not claimed to be an exactly equivalent rewriting of the Einstein equations.

The exact GR retarded Green-function tail motivates the causal support and hereditary architecture, but the coarse-grained exponential/multi-mode kernel is additional effective physics unless it is explicitly derived from the exact tail operator.

### GR limit

The Einstein term must remain present explicitly. A safe schematic form is

\[
G_{\mu\nu}=8\pi G\,T_{\mu\nu}+\Delta_{\mu\nu}[H,g,T],
\]

with

\[
\Delta_{\mu\nu}\to 0
\]

in the GR limit. The GR limit must never be implemented by multiplying the entire Einstein source by a kernel amplitude that vanishes as \(\eta_{\rm mem}\to0\).

Therefore the earlier manuscript ambiguity in which \(K\to0\) could imply \(G_{\mu\nu}\to0\) is prohibited in Stage 9.

## 2. Auxiliary/history sector: independent state until proven constrained

A first-order relaxation equation for a symmetric tensor history field enlarges the formal state space. Claims of "no new degrees of freedom" are not permitted unless the nonlinear constraint structure is explicitly demonstrated.

For Stage 9 the conservative interpretation is:

* the hereditary field/state is an **auxiliary dynamical state variable**;
* its retarded prescription or initial condition must be stated explicitly;
* its independent components, constraints, and projection structure must be tracked;
* the metric propagating spin-2 sector may remain unchanged even though the effective theory contains additional non-propagating or relaxational state variables.

This distinction is mandatory:

> "No new propagating metric poles" is not equivalent to "no new dynamical state variables."

## 3. Relation to exact GR tails

The Hadamard tail term

\[
G^{\rm ret}=U\,\delta_+(\sigma)+V\,\Theta_+(-\sigma)
\]

provides the causal inspiration for hereditary response. However, an exponential coarse-grained kernel with Gyr timescales or kpc coherence lengths is not to be described as derived from GR unless a quantitative coarse-graining calculation establishes that mapping.

Accordingly:

* exact GR tails = motivation and structural prior;
* finite-memory exponential/multi-mode kernel = effective ansatz unless derived;
* \(\tau_i\), amplitudes, projectors, advection law, and coherence scales may not be attributed to GR without a calculation connecting them to \(V_{\mu\nu\alpha\beta}(x,x')\).

## 4. Phenomenology claims

Stage 9 may test galactic dynamics directly, but claims about cosmology, lensing, or gravitational waves must be tiered by calculation level.

### Galactic dynamics

Allowed once the effective field equations, source/history law, metric coupling, normalization, and initial state are frozen before comparison with the halo target.

### Lensing

Requires a defined observable metric and a consistent calculation of the Weyl combination. Schematic statements alone are not sufficient for a precision claim.

### Cosmology

Requires a full linear perturbation treatment before precision claims about growth are made. Background FLRW evolution alone is a normalization/consistency calculation, not a complete cosmological test.

### Gravitational waves

If the Einstein kinetic operator is left unchanged, vacuum tensor propagation remains luminal at principal order. Any claimed frequency-dependent dispersion must be derived explicitly from the linearized effective equations; otherwise no such dispersion claim should be made.

## 5. Consequence for the current Stage 9 A0-A4 branch

The current branch is retained as exploratory scaffolding, but its interpretation changes:

* A0 state-frame advection is an effective history-state model.
* A1 metric coupling is an explicit effective modification and therefore resolves the referee's equivalence paradox by acknowledging changed observable dynamics.
* A2 derivative-only deposition is not part of the original tail paper and remains an alternative theory branch, not the canonical source law.
* A3's auxiliary/response field is consistent with the referee's concern only if its initial data and state-space enlargement are acknowledged.
* A4 external constraints remain necessary but cannot establish exact equivalence to GR.

## 6. Canonical Stage 9 theory fork

Before further Milky Way force generation, two source-state branches should be carried explicitly:

### Branch R: curvature-relaxation branch (closest to uploaded Paper 1)

\[
\tau\,D_U H_{\mu\nu}+H_{\mu\nu}
=\mathbb P_{\mu\nu}{}^{\alpha\beta}R_{\alpha\beta},
\]

with observable persistence built from a defined difference/coupling between the instantaneous curvature and the relaxing inherited state.

### Branch D: derivative-deposition branch (later Stage 9 alternative)

\[
D_U q_H+\frac{q_H}{\tau}
\propto D_U T.
\]

These are physically distinct models and must not be blended after seeing Galactic results.

## 7. Required gates before the next Milky Way force comparison

A candidate branch may generate a Stage 9 force only if all of the following are explicit and frozen:

1. effective-modification status;
2. Einstein/GR limit with the ordinary source term retained;
3. complete state variables and required initial data;
4. source/deposition or curvature-relaxation law;
5. observable metric coupling;
6. universal normalization parameters and provenance;
7. causal/state-frame evolution law;
8. no claim that an exponential kernel is literally derived from exact GR tails without a derivation;
9. no precision cosmology/GW claim without the corresponding perturbation calculation;
10. no parameter selected from Portail17/Hunter24, \(\Delta a\), orbit weights, SPARC, or any exposed Galactic halo target.

The referee report therefore does not terminate Stage 9. It clarifies what the test must be: a falsifiable, explicitly effective hereditary-gravity model inspired by GR tails, not a purportedly exact rewriting of GR that simultaneously changes observables.
