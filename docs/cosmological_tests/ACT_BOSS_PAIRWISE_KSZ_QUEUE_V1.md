## 3. Published measurement to reproduce

The study combines:

- ACT DR6 150-GHz/Planck 143-GHz CMB temperature mapping;
- the SDSS/BOSS galaxy correlation function;
- 227,837 selected luminous red galaxies in the redshift interval
  `0.44 < z < 0.66`; and
- pair separations of 30–230 Mpc.

The pairwise kinematic Sunyaev-Zeldovich (kSZ) signal traces the mean relative motion
of the electron-bearing halos. The published generalized radial dependence is

```text
g(r) proportional to 1 / r^n,
n = 2.1 +/- 0.3  (68% confidence).
```

Reported comparisons:

- Newtonian/GR-like `n = 2`: `chi^2 / dof = 20.1 / 15`, `PTE = 0.17`;
- deep-MOND-like `n = 1`: `chi^2 / dof = 33.8 / 15`,
  `PTE = 3.65e-3`;
- `n = 1` is disfavored by 3.3 sigma in the reported two-parameter analysis; and
- `n = 2` differs from the marginalized estimate by only 0.4 sigma.

The later DESI result cited in the media discussion is a 9.3-sigma detection of the
pairwise kSZ signal (Y. Gong et al., *Physical Review D* **113**, 063538 (2026),
<https://doi.org/10.1103/kkrh-1svk>). It must not be represented as a 9.3-sigma
rejection of MOND; the force-law comparison above remains the relevant published
constraint for this queue item.

## 4. Interpretation boundary

The paper primarily constrains the **shape** of the mean pairwise-velocity curve. Its
overall amplitude is marginalized through the effective mean optical depth of the
galaxy/halo sample. Therefore:

1. the result favors an approximately inverse-square effective acceleration field on
   the tested scales;
2. it disfavors the tested deep-MOND `n = 1` realization on those scales;
3. it does not uniquely identify the source of the additional effective gravitation as
   particle dark matter; and
4. a geometric/history-dependent source sector that is dynamically equivalent on
   these scales is not separated from dark matter by this statistic alone.

The published derivation assumes a statistically homogeneous and isotropic expanding
background, gravitationally generated halo peculiar velocities, a slowly varying
correlation function over the selected redshift slice, and a current-density/force-law
mapping. The MOND comparison does not simulate the external-field effect. These
assumptions and caveats must remain explicit in any project use.
