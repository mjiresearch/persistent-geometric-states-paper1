# Milky Way Stage 9 response-law boundary

Date: 2026-08-10

## Result of theory audit

The checked-in persistence manuscript already supplies the conceptual weak-field decomposition

\[
\mathbf a_{\rm dyn}=\mathbf a_b+\mathbf a_H+\mathbf a_{\rm int}+\cdots
\]

and requires causal evolution, independent initial data, finite relaxation, universal interaction structure, and continuous recovery of GR.

It also explicitly rules out interpreting the persistent state as a second instantaneous Poisson field sourced by the same static baryonic density.

However, the checked-in revision does not yet contain the explicit transport equation, hereditary deposition law, inherited/contemporary interaction law, or an independently frozen normalization needed to map the Stage 9 hereditary source cloud into a unique acceleration field.

## Consequence

Stage 9 must not presently compute

\[
\mathbf a_P(\mathbf x)
\]

by applying an arbitrary Newtonian/Poisson kernel to the memory cloud, nor by introducing a disk thickness, softening scale, coupling amplitude, or radial kernel selected after comparison with Portail17+halo, Hunter24+halo, Delta a(R), pulsar residuals, or orbit weights.

Doing so would add a response law that is not yet in the theory and could convert a halo-blind source reconstruction into a target-shaped force model.

## Required response-law gate

Before acceleration generation is allowed, a candidate universal response implementation must document and freeze, independently of halo targets:

1. causal transport/principal dynamics of the persistent degrees of freedom;
2. the hereditary deposition/source coupling generated by changing baryonic density/current rather than static presence alone;
3. finite relaxation/dispersion;
4. the interaction law between inherited and contemporary curvature, with interaction vanishing when either sector is absent;
5. continuous GR recovery;
6. universal coupling/normalization and any spatial correlation scale;
7. boundary and initial conditions needed for the Milky Way calculation.

The repository now enforces this requirement in `analysis/milky_way/persistence_response.py`.
