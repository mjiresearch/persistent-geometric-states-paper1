# Data policy

This repository is a public reproducibility archive for an independent research manuscript. It contains a mixture of project-generated products and observational inputs originating from external surveys and researchers.

## 1. Public third-party data

Third-party data are included only when their public release and redistribution terms permit it. Their original citations, provenance, and applicable terms remain in force.

## 2. Restricted or privately supplied data

Data supplied by a researcher through private communication, or otherwise not publicly redistributable, will not be committed to this public repository without explicit permission from the provider.

For such data, the public archive may retain:

- bibliographic/source provenance;
- acquisition status;
- dataset version or descriptive identifier;
- cryptographic checksums where appropriate and permissible;
- documented units and conventions;
- processing and validation code;
- descriptions of transformations;
- aggregate or derived scientific products where publication is permitted; and
- instructions for qualified researchers to request the source data from the appropriate provider.

The underlying restricted source files remain outside the public repository unless redistribution permission is granted.

## 3. No implied endorsement

Supplying observational data or answering a data request does not make the provider a collaborator and does not imply endorsement of:

- the Persistent Geometric States framework;
- the analysis choices made in this repository;
- the manuscript's interpretation; or
- any eventual empirical conclusion.

Authorship or collaboration is never inferred from data provision alone.

## 4. Frozen-analysis boundary

The repository uses versioned freeze records for empirical analyses. Observational sample membership, calibration/blind assignments, source-profile construction rules, and relevant validation procedures are established before final blind evaluation.

Privately supplied data may populate predeclared observational fields, but receipt of those data may not be used to alter frozen calibration/blind membership on the basis of model performance.

## 5. Derived products

Project-generated code, validation summaries, figures, statistics, and other derived products may be made public when doing so does not reconstruct or redistribute restricted source data contrary to the provider's terms.

If a derived product is sufficiently detailed that it would effectively reproduce restricted source material, it will also be withheld unless permission is obtained.

## 6. Provider requests

If a data provider specifies additional citation, acknowledgement, access, confidentiality, or redistribution conditions, those conditions will be documented and followed before the data are used in a public analysis.

## 7. Publication archive

The repository version associated with manuscript submission/publication will identify which inputs are public, which are restricted, and which derived outputs are publicly reproducible. A frozen archival release may subsequently be deposited in a DOI-bearing repository such as Zenodo.
