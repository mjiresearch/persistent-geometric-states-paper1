% ============================================================
\section{Baryonic Surface-Density and Source-Current Reconstruction}
\label{app:source-current}
% ============================================================

The stationary vector response depends on the present baryonic source current,
not only on the Newtonian baryonic acceleration. For each galaxy, the required
source is constructed from independently determined baryonic surface-density
profiles and the self-consistent circular velocity used by the stationary
field equation.

The stellar surface-density bases are obtained directly from the
inclination-corrected SPARC 3.6-$\mu$m disk and bulge surface-brightness
profiles. For nuisance mass-to-light ratios $\Upsilon_{d,g}$ and
$\Upsilon_{b,g}$,
\begin{equation}
    \Sigma_{\star,g}(R)
    =
    \Upsilon_{d,g}\,I_{d,g}(R)
    +
    \Upsilon_{b,g}\,I_{b,g}(R).
    \label{eq:appI-stellar-surface-density}
\end{equation}
The unit-$\Upsilon$ surface-density bases are archived before fitting so that
stellar nuisance parameters are not silently fixed during data construction.

The signed SPARC gas contribution is preserved in Newtonian baryonic
bookkeeping. Where the gas term enters a squared-speed sum, the convention is
\begin{equation}
    V_{\mathrm{gas}}^2
    \;\longrightarrow\;
    V_{\mathrm{gas}}\left|V_{\mathrm{gas}}\right|.
    \label{eq:appI-signed-gas-speed}
\end{equation}
This convention preserves negative tabulated gas contributions rather than
removing their sign by ordinary squaring.

The public SPARC mass-model release provides $V_{\mathrm{gas}}(R)$ but does
not itself provide the radial H\,I surface-density profiles. Because
$V_{\mathrm{gas}}(R)$ is a gravitational velocity contribution rather than a
surface density, it is not used as a proxy for $\Sigma_{\mathrm{gas}}(R)$.
The primary source-current analysis therefore uses independently sourced radial
H\,I profiles associated with the observational data underlying the SPARC mass
models. For each imported profile, the original reference, adopted distance,
inclination convention, radial units, interpolation rule, radial coverage, and
helium correction are archived before persistence fitting.

The neutral-gas surface density is constructed from the H\,I profile using the
globally frozen helium convention,
\begin{equation}
    \Sigma_{\mathrm{gas},g}(R)
    =
    f_{\mathrm{He}}\,
    \Sigma_{\mathrm{HI},g}(R),
    \label{eq:appI-gas-surface-density}
\end{equation}
with the adopted value of $f_{\mathrm{He}}$ declared in the frozen source-profile
manifest.

The total baryonic disk source is then
\begin{equation}
    \Sigma_{b,g}(R)
    =
    \Sigma_{\mathrm{gas},g}(R)
    +
    \Sigma_{\star,g}(R),
    \label{eq:appI-total-baryonic-surface-density}
\end{equation}
and the minimal stationary source current is
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{J}_g(R)
    =
    \Sigma_{b,g}(R)V_g(R).
    \label{eq:appI-source-current}
\end{equation}
Here $V_g(R)$ is the self-consistent velocity solved by the stationary field
equation. The observed velocity $V_{\mathrm{obs},g}(R)$ remains target data and
is not inserted into the source current.

A literature audit published after the original SPARC release reports radial
H\,I profiles for 169 of the 175 SPARC systems. Four systems lacking such
profiles occur in the frozen 149-galaxy stationary sample: D564-8, D631-7,
NGC~4138, and NGC~5907. Their handling was declared before any fit of
$L_A$ or $\mathcal{C}_A$. The primary direct-profile analysis excludes a system
only if an independently sourced radial H\,I profile remains unavailable at the
source-profile freeze. Any gravitational inversion used for such a system is
reported separately as a secondary reconstruction test and must first be
validated on galaxies with direct H\,I profiles.

The full machine-readable source basis, gas-profile provenance ledger,
interpolation metadata, validation products, and source-profile freeze record
are maintained in the Paper~I reproducibility repository under the Appendix~I
source-reconstruction directory. No $L_A$, $\mathcal{C}_A$, or $\tau_A$ value is
used to construct these products.
