# Ba05 / NGC4559 source statement

Primary source: Barbieri et al. (2005), *Extra-planar gas in the spiral galaxy NGC 4559*, A&A 439, 947-956, arXiv:astro-ph/0504534.

The paper reports WSRT 21-cm observations of NGC4559. The radial H I column-density profile is Figure 3 (right), derived from the observed total H I map by averaging column densities in ellipses using the kinematic position and inclination angles. The paper adopts distance 9.7 Mpc, mean inclination 67.2 deg, and states 1 arcmin = 2.8 kpc. Appendix A explicitly states that the gas column density used in mass modelling is obtained by multiplying the H I density by 1.4 for helium. Thus the source H I profile should be preserved as H I first; helium is a downstream convention.
