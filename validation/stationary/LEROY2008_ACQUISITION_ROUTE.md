# Leroy 2008 public H I profile acquisition route

Verified source: VizieR J/AJ/136/2782/table7, 687 radial-profile rows. The table provides galaxy name, galactocentric radius, SigmaHI, and e_SigmaHI. Per the catalog ReadMe, SigmaHI includes helium; the stationary hydrogen-only build must divide SigmaHI and e_SigmaHI by 1.36.

Frozen stationary targets still requiring this route: IC2574, NGC2403, NGC2976, NGC6946, NGC7331, NGC7793.

No SPARC observed velocities or halo targets may enter acquisition or harmonization.
